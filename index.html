Our Heritage

Where Royal Tradition Meets Contemporary Elegance

Established in 2012 in the heart of Jubilee Hills, Seven Star Restaurant was founded with a singular pursuit: to restore the vanishing royal culinary techniques of Awadh and the Deccan plateau.

Every single dish that leaves our earthen tandoor and copper handis reflects hours of meticulous marination, cold-pressed artisanal oils, and saffron handpicked from the valleys of Pampore.
